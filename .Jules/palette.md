## 2025-05-21 - [Micro-UX & Accessibility in Web Demo]
**Learning:** In dashboards with real-time updates, clear visual feedback for manual actions (like a spinning refresh icon) significantly improves perceived performance and reduces redundant clicks. ARIA labels on icon-only buttons are essential for screen reader users to understand non-textual controls.
**Action:** Always include `aria-label` for icon-only buttons and provide visual feedback (animation or disabled state) for async triggers.
